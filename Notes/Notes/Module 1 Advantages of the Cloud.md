## Trade capital expense for variable expense

- Capital expenses are used by companies to upgrade their infrastructure
- Instead of investing heavily before knowing how much will be used, cloud computing offers paying for only what you use
- Easily scalable
- Reduced Maintenance

## Benefit from massive economies of scale

- Lower cost because of many users
- Lower pay-as-you-go prices

## Stop guessing capacity

- Eliminating need for guessing capacity
- Scale up and down as required

## Increased speed and agility

- New resources are a click away
- Reduced timing for availability
- Cost and time for experimenting is significantly lower

## Expenditure on maintaining data centers

- Switches focus to the actual clients instead of infrastructure

## Global in minutes

- Deploy app in multiple in regions
- Lower latency at minimal cost
